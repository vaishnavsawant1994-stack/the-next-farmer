## Source validation fields
Every sourced content item must carry: publisher, canonical URL, title, crop and stage if relevant, state/district applicability, source publication date (if available), retrieved date, reviewer, version, confidence, reuse permission status, and next review date.

## Refresh and quality policy
- **Weather:** short TTL and clear 'last updated' date; do not render cached warnings as current.
- **Market prices:** exact mandi, crop, grade, timestamp and source; absent or stale data labelled.
- **Scheme pages:** periodic verification and immediate updates for important official notices.
- **Agronomy:** expert review at season/crop/regulatory changes; high-risk content needs stricter workflow.
- **Statistics:** always cite survey/reference year; never imply historical census numbers are current measurements.

## Research caveats
- 2015–16 Agriculture Census holding percentages and average 1.08 ha are historical reference values. The later census activity should not be misrepresented as published national results until verified.
- A 2026-08-07 government statement reported over 10.31 crore Farmer IDs **as of 2026-08-03**; that is a dated statement, not a live count.
- Official pages can change; website availability does not imply a documented public API.
- Farmer interviews and product usability findings are pending and cannot yet validate proposed feature priorities.
