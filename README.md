# The Next Farmer / द नेक्स्ट फार्मर

A farmer-first, multilingual digital agriculture ecosystem for India.

**Status:** Discovery and product-definition stage. This repository currently contains planning documentation, not a functioning production application.

## Mission
Make trustworthy, affordable, location-aware farming guidance available from crop planning to sale and payment, while helping farmers manage expenses and risk.

**Starting pilot:** Maharashtra (Nashik / Satana), with a design that can expand to other Indian regions after field validation.

## First principles
- Farmer outcomes before feature count; validate by field research.
- Marathi and Hindi first; voice, low-bandwidth and accessible UX.
- Support owner-farmers, tenants, women farmers, farm workers, livestock operators and FPOs without assuming land title.
- Show source, region, crop stage, publication date and confidence for recommendations.
- Never present uncertain photo diagnosis, pesticide dosage, chemical mixing, veterinary advice or price predictions as certain.
- Consent-based, minimal data collection; do not collect Aadhaar numbers, land records or bank details by default.
- Clear distinction between public information, estimates and expert-verified guidance.
